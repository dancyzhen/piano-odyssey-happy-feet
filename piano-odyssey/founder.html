Founder and director

Khew Yun Zhen

Pianist, educator, and dancer. Founder of The Piano Odyssey and Happy Feet.

Biography

Music, understood from within.

Khew Yun Zhen brings together an established career in piano performance and teaching, close to three decades of dance experience, and ongoing professional training in Dalcroze education.

As founder and director of The Piano Odyssey, she believes musical expression grows from musical understanding. Through listening, singing, movement, and creative exploration, she guides learners towards a deeper awareness of rhythm, phrasing, and musical character, and helps pianists bring that understanding into their playing.

Based in Holland Village, she offers personalised piano instruction, dedicated theory and aural lessons, accompaniment coaching, and collaborative piano services. She created Happy Feet to share music-and-movement learning beyond instrumental lessons, with a vision of programmes across ages and backgrounds.

Having studied Chinese dance and ballet since childhood, she brings an understanding of movement, coordination, and expressive gesture to her teaching. Her orchestral and chamber performance and collaborative piano experience inform her attention to listening, ensemble, and musical communication. She also creates performance opportunities for students through regular studio recitals.

Outside of music and movement, Yun Zhen enjoys baking.
