Teaching philosophy

A musical education rooted in experience.

I believe that learning music begins with developing a relationship with sound: listening attentively, discovering its character, and feeling how it moves us.

Before a musical idea becomes a symbol on a page or a movement of the fingers, it can be experienced through the voice and body. We can feel a pulse through walking, discover the shape of a melody through singing, and explore the direction of a phrase through movement.

Drawing on my experience as a pianist, dancer, and educator, alongside my ongoing Dalcroze training, I connect listening, singing, movement, and creative exploration to help learners understand music from within.

In piano lessons, those experiences become part of playing. A rhythm felt in the body can guide timing at the keyboard. A phrase understood through singing can guide breathing and direction. Technique and musical understanding develop together, giving students the means and intention to express themselves.

“We may sing the direction of a phrase, explore it through a gesture, and bring that sense of shape to the keyboard.” Khew Yun Zhen, on a typical moment in a lesson

Lessons and services

Four ways to work together.

  • Individual piano instruction

    For children, teenagers, and adults: beginners, returning players, and advanced students. Technique, repertoire, interpretation, theory, and aural work are integrated within each learner’s own musical journey.

    Enquire for current lesson lengths, fees, and availability

  • Specialist theory and aural lessons

    Dedicated sessions for musical understanding, listening, sight-singing, or focused exam support.

    These skills are also part of normal piano teaching, so piano students do not need to book them separately.

  • Accompaniment coaching

    For pianists learning to work with others: preparing scores, listening to partners, coordinating ensemble timing, balancing musical lines, and rehearsing effectively.

  • Collaborative piano services

    Professional accompaniment and ensemble collaboration for instrumentalists, singers, duos, and chamber groups, in exams, auditions, rehearsals, and performances.

    This is a performance service, distinct from coaching.

The founder

Pianist. Educator. Dancer.

Khew Yun Zhen is a pianist, educator, and dancer, and the founder of The Piano Odyssey and Happy Feet. Her teaching connects listening, singing, and movement with musical understanding and expression. She is currently working towards her Dalcroze Certificate.

She brings together an established career in piano performance and teaching, close to three decades of dance experience, and ongoing professional training in Dalcroze education.
