What a workshop can include

A four-hour option.

Each workshop is tailored to your group and space. As a guide, a four-hour session can move through these stages.

Teamwork is something participants practise together in the room. The workshop does not promise a guaranteed business outcome.

  1. Shared listeningArriving, tuning in to the music and to each other.
  2. Rhythm and coordinationStepping a pulse, passing patterns, and moving as a group.
  3. Small-group creative tasksShort pieces made together, where every voice shapes the result.
  4. A breakTime to rest and talk.
  5. ReflectionNoticing what helped the group listen, lead, and follow.
