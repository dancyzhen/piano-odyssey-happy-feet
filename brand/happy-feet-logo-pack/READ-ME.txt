HAPPY FEET - LOGO PACK
Music & Movement by The Piano Odyssey
Made 13 October 2026

TAGLINE
Step into the music

THE LOGO
The ball bounces along the name, each hop shorter, and lands on the two e's,
which is why they tip.

WHICH FILE TO USE
  Website, posters, flyers, documents
      happy-feet-logo-colour.png              on white or cream
      happy-feet-logo-white.png               on navy or dark photos
      ...-with-tagline.png                    adds the tagline "Step into the music"
      ...-with-piano-odyssey.png              adds "Music & Movement by The Piano Odyssey"
  Square spaces (stickers, T-shirts, stamps)
      happy-feet-logo-stacked-colour.png / -white.png
  Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp profile picture
      happy-feet-profile-navy-1080.png        recommended
      happy-feet-profile-cream-1080.png
      happy-feet-profile-sunflower-1080.png
  Facebook cover, email header
      happy-feet-banner-navy-1600x500.png
  App icon, browser tab, very small sizes
      happy-feet-icon-1024 / 512 / 180 / 32.png, happy-feet-icon.svg
  The bounce mark on its own
      happy-feet-mark-colour / -white (.png and .svg)

All logo PNG files have a transparent background, except the profile pictures,
banner and icons.

COLOURS
  Navy        #1E2A78   name, trail, backgrounds
  Sunflower   #FFC21A   the ball; the e's on navy
  Rose        #E8365F   the e's on white or cream
  Cream       #FBF7F2   light background

TYPEFACES (free from Google Fonts)
  DM Serif Display   the name
  DM Sans            the small line underneath

DO
  Leave clear space around the logo, about the height of the letter "h".
  Use the white version on navy or dark photos, the colour version on light.
  For anything smaller than about 3 cm wide, use the stacked logo or the icon:
  the dotted trail gets too faint.

DON'T
  Stretch or squash it, change the colours, straighten the e's, or put the
  colour version on a busy photo.

FOR A PRINTER
  The PNG files are large enough for A3 posters. A printer who needs a vector
  file of the full logo will need the name converted to outlines; ask and it
  can be prepared. The mark and icon SVG files are already true vectors.
